## ★★★★★ The last finding of the night, and the biggest one for the "is CIVVIS deciding" question

`residual: none` was structurally meaningless. It is copied into the turn record when
CIVVIS's orders ARRIVE, which is before most end-turn blockers fire, and the next
turn's reset wiped whatever was counted after. Every run in this project's history
reported `residual: none` for that reason.

With it emitted as its own event, attempt 6 at turn 30 reads:

    residual: {ENDTURN_BLOCKING_PRODUCTION: 10 (all @civvis),
               ENDTURN_BLOCKING_RESEARCH: 5, ENDTURN_BLOCKING_CIVIC: 3, ...}
    ⚠ PRODUCTION: CIVVIS chose 7, the built-in ladder chose 10 (58% of build decisions)

`ENDTURN_BLOCKING_PRODUCTION` routes into `driveProduction`, which picks the item
ITSELF from the hand-written ladder — it is not a pass re-running CIVVIS's order, and
`civ6_civvis_status` had been excusing it as one. Over whole runs: 76% of build
decisions on attempt 5, 44% on attempt 4. Attempt 5's most-built item was TEN
battering rams, reason `siege`, in a game with one rival met and no war.

**So the honest answer to "is CIVVIS deciding everything" is: units, research, civics,
policies, government, pantheon and settling — yes. Production — about half.** That is
the first thing to fix next, and it is not a one-line change: a blocker must be
answered or the turn never ends.

## ⚠ OPEN, AND THE LARGEST REMAINING CAUSE OF LOST TURNS: the dialogue clicks land and the screen does not close

Photographed live at 04:29 on attempt 6, stalled at turn 66:
`DiplomacyActionView` — Pericles, "We have sent you a delegation bearing gifts",
THREE options. The harness logged `[dialogue] window (864, 33, 864, 542)` and three
full passes over 20 positions, and the screen was still up four minutes later.

Everything checkable checks out, which is why this needs an experiment rather than
another guess:

- the window rect is RIGHT. Measured off the screenshot, the game window's left edge
  is at 864 points and its width is 864 — exactly what the harness read.
- the click positions are RIGHT. The three option buttons sit at fy 0.827, 0.869 and
  0.913 and fx ~0.17; the sweep walks fx 0.170, fy 0.60→0.94 in 0.02 steps, so each
  button is straddled by two clicks within ~5 points.
- the pointer really moves. `cliclick p` after the sweep reports (1010, 553), which is
  the last swept position — so Accessibility permission is granted and cliclick works.

So the clicks are delivered to the right pixels and Civilization VI does not act on
them. Things worth trying NEXT, in a scratch game rather than a ladder run:

1. a longer hold between mouse-down and mouse-up (`cliclick dd:` / `du:` with a delay)
   — some game UIs ignore a zero-length click;
2. `DiplomacyActionView_Expansion2`, not the base file, for a mod-side handler:
   [[civvis-civ6-controller-seven-bugs]] #10 records that the base file's
   `OnSelectConversationDiplomacyStatement` does not exist in the Expansion2
   replacement, so a shim added to the base one never fires;
3. a keyboard route — the shipped screen may accept Escape or a number key.

⚠ Do NOT test this by clicking at a live ladder run: on a map with no screen up, a
click selects a unit and the next one orders it to move.

### ...and that one is SOLVED, at n=1

`cliclick c:` sends mouse-down and mouse-up in the same instant, and Civilization VI's
dialogue buttons ignore it. One held press by hand —

    cliclick m:1011,528 ; sleep 1 ; cliclick dd:1011,528 ; sleep 1 ; cliclick du:1011,528

— and attempt 6 advanced from turn 66 to 67 immediately. `click_at` now holds for
120 ms (`m:` → `dd:` → wait → `du:`), which lands in attempt 7 onward.

⚠ n = 1. The harness's own stall watchdog had not fired when the held press landed, so
nothing else accounts for the recovery, but one observation is one observation. The
next stuck screen is the check.

⚠ The safe moment to experiment is WHILE STALLED — a screen is up, so a click cannot
select a unit. Never test a click against a running map.
