The first builds are listed best win rate first; press one to list what was built second after it, then third, eight builds deep. A row is CIVVIS’s win rate over the games that opened with exactly the builds down to it and, in brackets, those games. A build is a unit, building, district or wonder the capital’s production finished; one bought with Gold or Faith is not a build. Every finished game against the Deity AI on the run ledger counts, in a tournament or not: a win is a CIVVIS victory; a loss is a rival’s victory, our elimination, or a game conceded early.
