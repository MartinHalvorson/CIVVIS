How it is counted

Self-play games: six CIVVIS AI agents on a Continents map at Online speed, 250 turns, the newest finished batch. A seat with the forecasting rule on writes its plan down whenever the plan changes and at least every 17 turns, with the turn it expects to win on once it can support one. Counted here: every such note of a seat that won, made while it was going for the victory it won by. A note is placed by how many turns before the win it was written, ten turns to a point. The line is the middle forecast of each point and the band the middle half of them. Only a seat that won has a turn to be held to, so this says nothing of how sure a forecast makes a win.
