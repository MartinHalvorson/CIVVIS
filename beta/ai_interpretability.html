How it is counted

Self-play games: six CIVVIS AI agents on a Continents map at Online speed, 250 turns, the newest finished batch. A seat left to choose its own victory is not read. The seven numbers, at every 25th turn: cities, techs, civics, science and culture a turn, military strength, and wars. The reader learns the average empire of each aim from half the games, then names for each seat of the other half the aim whose average it is nearest to; the halves then change places. It is the plainest reader there is, so what it names is in the empires and not in the reader. Guessing would be right one time in six. Late in a game fewer seats are still playing, and only they are read. The reader · The agent read from inside, on Civ AI Interpretability
