A tournament is one idea, played out: a set number of verification games under fixed conditions, in which a CIVVIS AI agent plays inside Sid Meier’s Civilization VI against the game’s own AI. Tournaments are scheduled one after another, and the agents play them in order, game after game.

This copy of the page has no tournament record. It is written in when the site is published.

    Scheduling a tournament is adding its definition, the idea, the conditions and the number of games, to data/tournaments.json. It is listed here until its first game finishes.

    win   loss   A square is a game, in the order played; hover one for the civ, the ending and the turn. A win is any victory. A loss is a rival’s victory, our elimination, or a game conceded early. A launch that never reached a board is not a game. Dates are UTC, on the 24-hour clock. Wall time is from a tournament’s first game starting to its last game ending. Run time is the time its games were in play, added up, without the time between them. Hyperparameters are the settings every game of a tournament was played under.
