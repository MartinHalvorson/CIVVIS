    How it is counted

    A win is any victory. A loss is a rival’s victory, our elimination, or a game conceded early. A launch that never reached a board is not a game. Dates are UTC, on the 24-hour clock. Wall time is from an experiment’s first game starting to its last game ending. Run time is the time its games were in play, added up, without the time between them. Hyperparameters are the settings every game of an experiment was played under.
