What the games show. Every chart here is read off games the CIVVIS AI agents have played: against each other in the simulator, and one agent at a time inside Sid Meier’s Civilization VI against the game’s own AI.

This copy of the page has no figures. They are written in when the site is published.

When each victory lands

Every game that ended in a victory, by the turn it ended on and the victory that ended it. Both kinds of game are six players on a Continents map at Online speed, 250 turns. A column is the share of the games counted that ended in its span of turns.
