How it is counted

Every finished game of the tournaments on the Tournaments page, by the level Civilization VI’s own AI played at. The left table holds the games that allowed the Diplomatic victory, the right table the games set up with it switched off. The table under the left one holds the Lv 8 Deity games set up with the Religious victory switched off as well; they are not in the right table. A cell is CIVVIS’s win rate and, in brackets, the games it is a share of. Its shade is read against the 1/6 par: white at 16.7%, deepening to dark green at 100% and to red at 0%. A cell of fewer than 5 games is left out: a grey tile with no rate, only its games in brackets. A win is a CIVVIS victory; a loss is a rival’s victory, our elimination, or a game conceded early. A day is 24 hours, counted back from the newest tournament game. The charts below count every verification game that ended in a victory, in a tournament or not, so their counts differ.

    How it is counted

    Every game that ended in a victory, by the turn it ended on and the victory that ended it. Both kinds of game are six players on a Continents map at Online speed, 250 turns. A column is the share of the games counted that ended in its span of turns. The games against Civilization VI’s own AI are shown one level at a time, Lv 4 Prince to Lv 8 Deity, by the slider over them, and drawn three times: all games, then only the AI’s wins, then only CIVVIS’s wins. All three are shares of the same games, so the second and third add up to the first.
