How it is measured

  1. Human player One person plays one game of Civilization VI.
  2. CIVVIS computer control CIVVIS plays the real Civilization VI against its Deity AI.
  3. Firaxis Civ 6 Deity sim The same game in the simulator, on one core: a CIVVIS seat against five Deity rivals that play as Civilization VI’s AI does.
  4. CIVVIS self-play One simulator game on one core, every seat a CIVVIS AI agent.
  5. M5 Max, all 18 cores Self-play on every core of one M5 Max: the time from one finished game to the next.
  6. Theoretical A million games in the time a person plays one.
  7. Theoretical A billion games in the time a person plays one.
