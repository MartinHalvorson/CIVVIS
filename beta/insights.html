    How it is counted

    Every game that ended in a victory, CIVVIS’s or the AI’s, by the turn it ended on and the victory that ended it: one CIVVIS AI agent inside Civilization VI against the game’s own AI, six players on a Continents map at Online speed, 250 turns. A column is the share of the level’s games that ended in its five turns, and the turn marked is the median: half the level’s games were over by it. A higher level is a stronger AI: Civilization VI gives its AI bigger bonuses at each level, not a different way of deciding. Games against the AI · The same games on Win Analytics
