# Costed Pingala launch relocation (opt-in)

`pingala-earns-the-launch` is an independent, default-off experiment. An explicit
Science target can send established Pingala to a current space project when a
forecast, including establishment and displaced production, saves a full quoted
turn. Magnus can return to the vacated city through the existing native governor
exchange continuation. Ordinary governor behavior remains unchanged with this
flag off. No deployment or native-strength promotion is made.

Native Indonesia pilot `civvis-20261105T184951Z-campus-sea-pilot-v2-03-on` completed
its Spaceport at141 and satellite at152. At the loss on166, Moon Landing held
686/750 production with two turns left. Space Initiative was present by134 but
Pingala remained in the capital while the launch city held Magnus/Industrialist.
The original game enabled `pingala-follows-research`, not its second version.
Version two rejects remote major wars, temporary non-Science posture and any
specialist Magnus. This experiment prices the latter and follows the assigned
Science target across temporary postures. The earlier version-two evaluation
and its artifacts remain intact in `2026-11-05-pingala-launch.md`.

The new prepass precedes title spending so the native continuation can retain
its reciprocal assignment pair. Recovery, local threats, loyalty protection,
disabled or immature posts, same-turn requests, an existing Spaceport at the
old post, and Curator works retain their guards. Other specialist/defensive
occupants keep their posts. A Spaceport under construction earns no project
bonus. Native remaining-turn quotes calibrate remaining work; relative modeled
production prices both moving governors' absence during establishment and their
established effects afterwards, including Magnus's plant/regional production.

A separate experimental budget permits at most5% loss of the empire's science
plus half its culture over the projected completion interval. It is an untuned
hypothesis. Great Person points, future projects, changing economies, and the
complete victory schedule are not priced. Native evaluation is still needed.
