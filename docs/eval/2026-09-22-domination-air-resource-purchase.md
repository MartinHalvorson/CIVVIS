# Connect the first Bomber resource

Native083707 researched Advanced Flight on turn 151 and had a completed Aerodrome, but its Aluminum stock and income remained zero through the continuation’s Culture loss to the Aztecs on turn 235. The revealed deposit at native coordinates (23,15) remained unowned beside our border, within three hexes of Cumaná at (25,13). On continuation turn 201, the snapshot held 151 Gold, positive income and a charged Builder two tiles away. These snapshots establish an opportunity to investigate, not the actual purchase-phase quote or treasury.

The resource-purchase exception previously only answered an existing melee or siege unit’s researched upgrade. It could not supply the first Bomber. Extend that same exception when a civilization-specific Bomber is researched, its required district family has an unpillaged completed instance, and its visible strategic resource has zero stock and income. Reuse all existing legal plot, owned-source, Builder charge, route, destination risk, treasury reserve and threatened-home guards. This does not start a research beeline or buy fuel for an unbuilt field.

The first prototype retained the ground-purchase strategy gate and made no change to either native replay (151 continuation frames and 339 control frames). Diagnostic instrumentation reproduced all 151 replies exactly. It showed no legal Aluminum purchase quote on turns 197, 201 or 203. On turn 231 the planner entered Recovery with 228 Gold, then later entered Conquest with only 63 Gold against a 175 Gold plot quote. The continuation therefore does not support the earlier raw-snapshot affordability hypothesis.

The revised implementation preserves Conquest/Expansion restrictions for ground upgrade demand while allowing researched Bomber fuel demand during recovery and rival-victory counter plans. The existing emergency purchases still run first; threatened-home and treasury guards still apply. Two additional regressions cover air demand in Recovery/Diplomacy and unchanged ground restrictions. All eleven focused tests pass. Revised release replay and integration validation are pending. No Bomber completion, faster capture or native Domination victory is claimed.
