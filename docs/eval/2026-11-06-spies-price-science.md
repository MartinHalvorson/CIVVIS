# Spy producers follow the native completion quote

`spies-price-the-science-head` is an independent default-off experiment. Both the Eureka-steal and science-denial training claims keep their existing eligible producer set, then use the earliest native remaining Spy quote. On an exact quote tie, a non-science queue precedes a Campus-family district or building; raw Production and city id break remaining ties. It runs only on an assigned Science seat when **every eligible candidate** has a finite, positive host quote. Otherwise the complete original Production/id ordering remains. No agent is cancelled or redirected; capacity, pause, threat, launch-city and queue eligibility still belong to the existing callers.

The original Indonesia012732Z audit identified a real conflict: Ternate's Campus held 76/116 production while successive Spies trained, with a seven-turn Campus proposal displaced at149. Removing denial alone from fixed-input proposals still selected a Spy through the Eureka training claim. That does not establish that the Spies were a bad investment. Successful Eureka steals have been a substantial source of Science. An untested prototype that delayed Spies behind short science construction was rejected before commit because it did not price those returns. The implemented experiment instead preserves the earliest **quoted** Spy completion and spares science only on ties.
