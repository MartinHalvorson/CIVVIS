# Science prerequisites across the rolling research window

`science-chain-crosses-window` is a new, unpriced opt-in. It ships off and
inherits no other gene's rating. No native game, waiting harness, deployment
force list or default selection is changed by this work.

## Native witness

Ordinary Indonesia/Gitarja, Deity, Online, assigned-Science run
`civvis-20261105T215700Z` was a natural rival Science loss at turn193. At129/0,
the native tech tree marked Flight and Replaceable Parts as legal. Both are
prerequisites of Rocketry. The source's oldest incomplete era was2 and its
three-era window ended at4; both legal prerequisites were era5. The legal
native technologies inside that window were Machinery, Ballistics and Castles,
none on Rocketry's prerequisite tree. The controller emitted Machinery and its
retained journal says it chose that technology by its generic score.

The adjacent `.native-case.json` binds the source, raw archive, exact state,
canonical decision, source technology data and journal hashes. Its scope is
native legality plus source candidate bounds. It does not prove the complete
persistent agent's internal forced-goal state or a different native outcome.

## Change

When this opt-in is on, an explicitly assigned Science controller admits the
legal steps toward its selected Science milestone outside the ordinary era
window. Admission happens after the existing goal ordering. A higher-priority
wall, appointed breakthrough, military modernization or other selected goal
therefore keeps its slot. Every added technology must be legal and an ancestor
of the selected milestone; the existing scorer chooses among those steps.
An ongoing research project is not cancelled. Adaptive or other assigned
victory contracts keep their ordinary window.

The full chooser regression uses the37 native known technology names and era
in a small model fixture. Other fields are synthetic. It is a model routing
regression, not a reconstruction of the native board or a strength experiment.
