# Commit observed movement memory only for executed steps

The eight-map simulator comparison of the native controller revision a7ba7f5 with accumulated Domination fixes through 300af974 produced no focal wins in either arm. Both held zero foreign capitals. The later arm held six foreign cities across the maps, versus seven before; these counts include city-states. This sample did not establish stronger overall play.

Tracing the later arm's seed 37330001 exposed an execution defect. The game ended in a Science loss on turn 215, with no captures and only three focal attacks on city centers. Trebuchet 507 remained at (13,27) through turns 122–130 despite a siege assignment and legal steps toward its firing position.

On turns 122 and 123, the observed-player adapter executed a finishing volley and requested a fresh observation before ordinary movement. Ordinary movement had already been planned on a disposable board. Its controller path history nevertheless retained the projected walk through (12,27). The next frame therefore rejected the still-unexecuted first step as a same-turn reversal. After two turns, the refusal watcher treated the unissued walk as host-refused and barred that step until turn 130. Diagnostic logging of plans, siege state, movement refusals and execution preserved the complete result JSON and every action byte of the original game.

The repair snapshots path history before each observed frame. The executor records actual source and destination positions for successful Move and MoveTo actions. After execution, the controller restores earlier committed path history and appends only those successful steps. Current-frame speculative refusal watches are replaced by watches for executed movement; judgments based on prior observations remain intact. This also handles a partially executed batch while retaining reversal protection for movement already taken.

Four focused regressions fail against a no-op reconciliation and pass with the repair: discarded projected walks, partially executed walks, false refusal accumulation from unissued walks, and a later cancelled frame after earlier real movement. Existing execution tests retain their original entry points.

The change belongs to the observed-player simulator adapter and applies to opponents as well as the focal seat. It does not change the native Civilization VI executor. Simulator wins, if subsequently observed, do not establish a native Domination victory.
