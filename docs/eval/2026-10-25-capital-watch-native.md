# Existing capital-watch timing across all sixteen Native starts

The existing wall policy was enabled in all sixteen Immortal starts, and its
decision journal appears in seven. The complete [Native trial](2026-10-25-culture-air-native-screen.md)
has zero verified wins. This posthoc audit finds two distinct problems among
the nine original-capital loss markers: **two occur before turn 100 without
completed walls; seven occur later after walls already stood**. Earlier wall
preparation cannot by itself explain most of these losses.

The method was recorded before aggregate computation, after outcomes and the
turn-34 exploratory case were known. All sixteen starts remain included.
No gameplay, registration, default or rating is changed.

These are observed turns, not estimated completion dates. The broad public
power hint occurs in every game, including all seven without a capital-loss
marker, and may describe a different or distant major. Eight games have the
table's current exported-unit signal. The actual policy additionally carries
sighting memory and reads the controller's own vision. A missing table signal
does not imply the policy could not fire: start 195822 has a real policy
journal entry despite no matching first-state current-unit signal.

In start 193342 the actual policy requests Masonry at turn 21, reporting
India at 2.8x our power. Native research orders/readback confirm that choice;
Masonry becomes known at turn 31, after Bogotá's turn-26 loss and before the
turn-34 elimination. In start 185658 Masonry is known at 31, but the wall
queue is first observed at 47, before the turn-48 loss. The first case needs
more preparation lead; the second already had the technology and needs its
queue/defense handoff examined. Neither is evidence for a missing enable.

All seven later markers occur after walls have been observed. Bogotá in
start 193612 is observed again in our local roster at turn 196 after its
turn-182 loss marker; the audit does not infer the mechanism of its return.
Several losses have zero damage on the last held observation. Native exports
between our decisions do not cover every rival action, so that does not rule
out combat or establish a loyalty cause. Loss markers are events, not an
assertion that all nine capitals remain lost at the terminal state.

The authoritative census corrects an initial readback scope error: Masonry
completion is tracked even after the original capital is lost. The initial
source/report/log remain in the evidence archive. Two regression controls
verify research after loss without adopting a replacement city's walls, and
exported troop presence without current plot sight. Both pass; six existing
wrapped-geometry/roster controls also pass. Exact cohort coverage, every
event-file hash and every loss event match the published trial.
