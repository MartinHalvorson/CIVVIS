# Prospective 48-start Native forward-airfield trial

Prospective Native verification of the default-off forward-airfield project.

Do not arm before the corrected feature is merged, required checks pass and
the actual delivered adapter is qualified from an unarmed game. This is a new
trial. The completed Slinger trial and its observer remain closed.

Register forty-eight future original attempt stems under the unchanged production
ladder. Deterministic driver arms are not balanced or replaced. Keep every
failed start, game exit, retirement, continuation and overrun. The primary
endpoint is the actual local seat's Native outcome and victory type. Unknown
host endpoints remain unknown. Record actual rung and lane in every attempt.

Before activation, record the actual unarmed driver, delivered source ancestry,
exact binary bytes and first complete captured request. Require full-profile
OFF reproduction of its first decision and only the candidate bit changing ON;
retain both responses and every captured read. Installed Lua can be hashed;
loaded historical identity remains unattested unless independently proved.

Copy the tested observer and protocol outside the task worktree. Create the
shared gene lease exclusively. At forty-eight starts remove only its unchanged
owned inode/content identity. Preserve another owner's or replacement lease.
Never stop or restart the shared driver, alter its rung, or rearm an old trial.

Measure the played arm, candidate journal, actual emitted order and subsequent
Native execution separately. An offered stage or model-applied district is
not construction. A later production readback or district foundation can
support an issued stage; a completed Encampment/Barracks-or-Stable/Armory,
Engineer spawn, actual Engineer travel, completed Airstrip, Bomber rebase,
city damage, original-major-capital possession and conquest award each need
their own Native observations. Preserve missing or ambiguous joins. Host quote
p is offered-stage turns; n is placement count. Do not infer whole-chain ETA,
arrival, visibility, attacks or capture from that single quote.

Track the project by stable city positions across rebuilds and retain its
original finite deadline. Do not alter stage, route, budget or safety bounds
from observed outcomes. No source changes, default promotion or ledger
promotion belong to this experiment. Retain all source/binary/profile drift;
small mixed-source results do not establish a causal strength effect.

Coordinate joins must convert journal/model axial positions through the actual
src/hex.rs axial_to_offset rule before matching Native offset x/y, including
map wrapping. Local state city rosters replace previous holdings; a captured
or lost city is not retained as owned. Explicit tiles events replace complete
plot records; delta metadata is not a fresh plot observation. Unknown ownership,
missing placement lists and missing pipeline stages remain unknown.

The production ladder independently gained low-Production retirement in #4168
(main ff783b414dddb2476013bdd71bdb50a97dd103a6). Record the actual driver's
--restart-below-leader-production (or its policy-file setting), existing rank
threshold and retirement readbacks in qualification and every
attempt. Preserve such retirements without a host outcome as unknown, never
as an observed defeat or an excluded replacement. This trial does not change
the shared retirement policy or restart a game to obtain a different endpoint.

Before registration or arming, the draft sixteen-start plan was replaced with
forty-eight starts because the independently enabled one-third Production
retirement rule can stop games before Flight. This cutoff is fixed before any
new trial assignment or candidate outcome. Keep every start and do not extend,
rebalance or replace it in response to outcomes. The earlier sixteen-start
preparation and its controls remain retained as draft history.
