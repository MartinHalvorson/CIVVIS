# Native siege queue funding experiment

In `civvis-20261009T040435Z`, Bogotá repeatedly selected a Shrine before the native queue-restoration pass countermanded it with its invested Bombard at turns 105 and 106. At turn 105 the native treasury held 24 Gold with net income -3.6875; the Bombard had 119 of 140 Production invested. At turn 106 the bank held 21 Gold and income -5.58984. The new Bombard, native ID 5963786, first appeared at turn 107 with 100 HP and 4 Gold maintenance, when the bank held 15 and income was -10.6914. It remained at 100 HP through turn 109, with Gold 0 and income -17.7891. The `unit_lost` event at turn 110 reported Gold -18. No recorded combat event named this unit. Bankruptcy is an inference; the native removal event does not export that cause.

The campaign still lacked a fast enough wall breach at turn 149: Addis Ababa had 300/300 walls and 200/200 city health, and the trace reported a staged damage budget of infinite turns against 6.6 turns of endurance. The game took Ethiopian non-capitals Lalibela at 91 and Sodo at 127, lost Sodo at 138 after observed loyalty fell to 3.3125, and captured no major capital. It retired at 150 with production rank 3, production 192 against 235/130/333, and 40 kills / 33 combat losses. The combined preceding source `5e8e0fba98c4b964f17c0e22550812e5282537c3` completed **0/4**, all production-rank retirements. None is removed from the non-win denominator.

A fresh-controller replay of the turn-105 first state chose Expansion and did not reproduce the active Conquest queue-restoration pass. It therefore supplies no causal comparison of that pass. The recorded native actions do show the Shrine order followed by the Bombard order. The Shrine is a religious-source choice, not a Gold-building choice. A separate Basic governor diagnostic selected a Commercial Hub under the quoted budget. That diagnostic is not a native Hub order or proof of recovery.

The experimental patch keeps an existing nonmilitary queue while the governor's existing treasury-recovery condition holds: income below -0.5 and Gold below `100 + 25 * owned_city_count`. The restoration pass cannot then force another gun's upkeep over an already selected infrastructure, civilian or religious-source queue. A positive income, a bank at or above the reserve boundary, an empty queue, or an ordinary military replacement retains the earlier restoration behavior. Existing local-defense and peace protections remain upstream. Saved gun Production is retained and can resume after recovery.

The uncorrected source failed the recovery-queue regression by restoring Catapult instead of Granary; the funded-deficit, positive-income and military-replacement controls passed. The corrected source passed those four cases plus the native-shaped reserved Shrine case, including preserved invested gun Production. Earlier fixture compilation and missing-recovery setup failures are not credited as the regression witness. Retained native gate validation and the release build are in progress. The correction has not yet actuated in a native game, and no survival, production or winning gain is established.
