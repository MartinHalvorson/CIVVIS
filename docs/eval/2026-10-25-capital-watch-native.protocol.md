# Capital warning timing: posthoc full-cohort audit

Use all sixteen assigned starts from the completed Culture-air Native trial
in PR #4141, including every elimination. Outcomes and the turn-34 defeat are
already known. This is a posthoc diagnostic census, not a prospective
experiment or a causal win estimate. The exploratory turn-34 case shows the
existing enabled policy selecting Masonry at turn 21, war by 23 and loss of
Bogotá at 26. Do not select only that case or replace unfavorable cases.

Before aggregating, record the following for every game: first met major at
peace with at least twice our public military power, first such major's
exported land soldier within eight wrapped hexes of the original capital,
the corresponding current plot-visibility observation, actual policy research
journal entries, Masonry requests/readback, wall queue/readback, first capital
damage and original-capital loss. Retain full turn/frame rows and raw source
hashes. Separate early losses from later losses and retained capitals. Show
unknown readings explicitly.

Use complete fresh city rosters and whole plot-record replacement. Classify
major/minor original founders from observed rosters. The current holder does
not classify an original founder. Host-exported unit presence, plot visibility
and fresh adapter visibility are distinct; these field comparisons are not
an exact emulation of the controller's persistent sighting memory. Native
first-state rows can precede the actual decision input, and orders/journals
are not execution proof. Record actual requested orders and later host
readbacks separately. Do not invent wall completion timings or infer that
earlier research would have prevented a loss.

No gameplay or default change is included. Any candidate derived from this
audit needs its own controls and outcome verification.
