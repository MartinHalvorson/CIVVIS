# One forward-airfield project: development and validation protocol

The complete fixed eight Emperor material and sixteen Immortal Culture-air
games are already known zero-win samples. Their range/terrain and support-chain
audits motivate this candidate; they are not fresh strength evidence. Keep
all old games and all candidate failures. Do not replace them with the selected
host-awarded Emperor Conquest win.

Implement one default-off `forward-airfield-project` opt-in, confined to an
assigned Domination seat with a healthy standing two-Bomber wing, Flight and
Military Engineering, an active war against a known major original capital
that no current own base reaches, and an observed candidate field in range of
both planes. No generic military or declaration threshold is changed.

Preparation uses only normal legal production offers, one producer at a time:
Encampment, Barracks or Stable, Armory, Engineer. Prefer useful existing
prerequisites over a new chain. Begin only from an idle queue after existing
emergency, economy and victory reservations. Protect the accepted chain from
routine rescoring; Recovery, insolvency, threats, illegal offers and a changed
objective release it. Do not buy unavailable units, assume future menus, or
claim a complete Native ETA from a quote for the next stage.

The implementation must state its finite production/travel budgets and upkeep
reserve as hypotheses before measuring changed proposals. Native quotes keep
their original semantics: `p` is turns left, `n` is placement count. No new
research route or generic Encampment bonus. No neutral site is treated as owned,
no missing observation as permission, and no geometry as proof of a safe route.

An eligible Engineer keeps civilian/formation locks and existing emergency
work priority. It may take only a known, legal, safe next step and builds only
through the ordinary legal improvement action. Future route safety is checked
again each frame. The field and wing still have to satisfy normal native slot,
rebase and attack permissions. A production proposal is not a completed stage
or an executed airstrip.

Run meaningful regressions first against a genuine no-op helper, retain RED,
then implement. Cover OFF, Recovery, active/foreign/minor capital filtering,
missing prerequisites, one project, busy and protected queues, invalid/native
offers, nonfinite/negative income, upkeep, unsafe/foreign/unseen terrain and
Engineer movement/building limits. Check both production routes and final
output preservation, not only a selector's return value.

Use all twenty-four fixed Native games for a dated captured-input comparison,
with complete captures, unchanged rival profiles, OFF reproduction controls and
exactly the candidate bit varied. Retain every failed query. Fresh stateless
queries are proposal evidence and do not replay historical controller memory
or Native outcomes. A registered eight-map focal-only Immortal comparison uses
seeds 59012600 through 59012607, four players, 60x38 Pangaea, six city-states,
Online, all victories enabled, 300-turn cap and three fixed Firaxis-mix rivals.
Restore the entire rival genomes in each pair and verify original-world
reproduction. Keep full canonical actions, outcomes, original-capital and home
capital measures, and journal-drop limits. This small probe cannot promote a
default by itself.

A future Native screen requires actual delivered-source/binary/profile controls
and its own registered finite observer. The currently running Slinger trial
keeps its exclusive lease; never overlap, replace or rearm its cutoff.

Before proposal measurement, initial finite hypotheses are: at most sixteen
standard turns for any offered stage, forty standard turns for the preparation
reservation, and at most twelve known land edges from producer to field.
Current income must cover a conservative six Gold/turn for the complete
Encampment/Barracks-or-Stable/Armory/Engineer chain, while cash preserves the
existing 1.5-turn upkeep reserve including that allowance. The travel bound is
a known-land planning bound, not an asserted future Native travel time.

The pre-query selector is now fixed over the authoritative terrain census:
213 unique actual decisions, 247 distinct captured reads, all twenty-four
games. The 254 blocked capital-turns share 165 actual frames; those are read
once each, with all affected capitals retained, plus first/final decisions
from every game. The exact manifest and source hashes are archived with the
comparison. Repeated fresh OFF queries must reproduce each other. Compare OFF
with historical output separately and retain mismatches: a stateless current
controller is not the old process's persistent history.

Before implementing the longer chain, also describe every actual offered
Aerodrome placement in all 254 fixed blocked capital-turns. An owned district
can differ from an Airstrip's terrain/feature permissions; use the offered
placement list and each ready Bomber's actual range, not an empty-land proxy.
Keep no-offer, missing-site-list and out-of-range cases. The six already-fixed
earliest witnesses have now been inspected exploratorily and have zero
in-range offered Aerodrome sites. This is not the full census result.
Register this alternative check before its complete aggregation. No treated
query has run, and no whole-game outcome will choose the validation seeds.


At the next registration, the completed one-shot pass retains all 213 cases /
247 reads, all exact-genome and OFF-repeat controls, and zero changed orders.
Six registered diagnostic witnesses are being inspected without policy edits.
Source inspection identifies a material harness difference: the one-shot path
creates a fresh opening book; `--serve --fresh-board` explicitly skips the
opening when its first state is past turn one and adopts current host queues.
The feature hook belongs after the opening. The one-shot null does not prove
that a persistent production controller could never activate the policy.

Before any corrected-path proposal is measured, retain the same complete
213-case / 247-read selection, all twenty-four games and both unchanged source
binaries. Run fresh `--serve --fresh-board --explain` OFF, ON and OFF-repeat
processes with one request at the captured turn, then EOF. Preserve all raw
responses, stderr, commands, full played-genome readback and failures. Require
all three successful responses, exact noncandidate genomes and identical
repeated OFF orders. Compare with historical orders separately. These are
production-path cold starts, still not replay of historical persistent state.
Use six previously fixed stage witnesses for stderr-only gate diagnostics;
accept observations only where the entire response and genome equal the
uninstrumented arm. No production threshold or outcome seed changes.


Before live deployment, cover project persistence through city renumbering.
The production bridge rebuilds city IDs while keeping coordinates stable;
the first prototype stores IDs and has no city-memory remap. Add two tests
against unchanged policy: a held offered stage follows the same producer and
capital after a rebuild, and an expired project cannot restart its finite
budget merely because those IDs move. Retain the genuine failing results,
then key this new reservation by stable city positions. No stage, route,
budget or safety threshold changes. Frozen-source proposal/model evidence
remains labeled with its original source; new source needs its own checks.
