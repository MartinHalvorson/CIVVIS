# Parallel first Library and backlog Builder — unqualified experiment

`parallel-library-backlog` is an independent default-off scheduling experiment for an assigned Science seat's opening. It coordinates two existing claims without changing their jobs, eligibility, Builder quota or build budgets. It introduces no valuation credit or Civilization VI rule.

In the completed ordinary Indonesia run `civvis-20261106T052942Z`, the capital's Library was offered at35 for4 turns but first observed queued at74. Functionally matching replays through80 reproduced195 complete native requests and their exact captured input sequences in each of two controls. The original launch setup was reconstructed, so original kernel argv/cwd/runtime identity is not claimed. Two diagnostic-only builds independently kept those same payloads and reads unchanged. Their predicates showed protected Settlers, military and development queues, including the backlog Builder, blocking the Library claim. This is one-world mechanism evidence, not independent samples or recovered turns.

At66/frame0 the existing claims choose a Builder in the capital (native quote3) and a first Library in Palembang (quote6). Both cities are initially idle and meet both claims' existing guards. The alternate native quotes are Library4 in the capital and Builder5 in Palembang. Swapping the jobs reduces the quoted time until both are ready from6 to5, with the same sum9. Research is quoted2 turns earlier, the Builder2 turns later. This is a tradeoff, not a Pareto improvement of both completion times or a strength result. Later queue writers, policy changes and host execution can invalidate the forecast.

The opt-in reuses the original claims' selectors. It requires the existing Campus-building claim, backlog claim and science-head protection, an assigned Science target before the existing specialization boundary, and two idle producers. The original fastest Builder city must also be the preferred first-Library city. Excluding it, the original Builder and first-Library selectors must agree on the partner. All four native quotes must be finite and positive. The swap must improve both-jobs-ready time, make the Library earlier and not increase the sum of quoted completion times. Absent quotes or a failed condition leave the old ordering intact.

The preferred city reserves its Library just before the normal backlog claim. Science-head protection then sends that unchanged Builder claim to its partner. The later Campus-building claim is skipped only while the early Library remains at its head, retaining one final Campus-building claim; if another intervening writer replaces it, the ordinary claim can still run. Existing safety, attack, housing/Granary, Settler and protected-head restrictions remain in the shared selectors. The full hint/production chain and subsequent Spy, Plaza and policy outputs must be reviewed before advancement.
