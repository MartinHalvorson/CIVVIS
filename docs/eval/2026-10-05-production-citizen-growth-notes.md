# Native early citizen growth experiments

The first consumed diagnostic adds only +2 food appetite to the existing public
per-city directive channel in young cities with housing and no nearby threat.
The public targeted Domination live bridge controller has city_strategy off,
which is asserted, so this does not overwrite another directive. Both arms
forward the ordinary observation contract. Public weights, engine costs and
yields, protected live pin and controller, registry, and deployment defaults
are unchanged. This is a native assignment policy; no Firaxis assignment or
parity is established.

All sixteen executions exited zero. Production at turn 75 fell 1.30% at Emperor
and 1.21% at Deity, and Deity Science failed the 90% guard. Turn-75 survival was
unchanged. The first policy is rejected. All eight control action logs and
final worlds reproduce the preceding opening study's controls byte for byte.
Eligible directives and actual food weights are read back from every city each
turn. The initial probe compile caught an unparenthesized cast comparison
before any play; the corrected optimized probe was frozen before all games.
All thought IDs were contiguous, with no resets or truncated turns.

The next prototype uses a per-city payback forecast before choosing food. It
will remain a separately frozen consumed diagnostic; no fresh pilot or
confirmation is played on the basis of the first result.
