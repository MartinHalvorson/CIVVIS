# A revealed launch technology can replace an obsolete native research path

Native Deity/Online Korea Science loss `civvis-20261105T103825Z` demonstrates a research-slot problem after revelation. The journal requested Advanced AI at turn 176 toward the still-hidden Smart Materials. The native `GetResearchPath` operation studied Seasteads first. At 179/0 Seasteads was owned and Smart Materials was explicitly revealed with only Seasteads as prerequisite and `can_research: true`, but the automatically queued Advanced AI had started with zero invested science. At 180 it held 383.773 science. The planner waited for that node to finish before selecting Smart Materials at 183. This is not a counterfactual four-turn victory gain: Advanced AI could still be needed later, and launch production also constrained this loss at 190.

The off-by-default `revealed-launch-tech-takes-the-slot` treatment reconsiders an active native path only for the next committed late launch milestone. Its complete prerequisites must be observed, current host `CanResearch` must explicitly be true, it must be legal in the reconstructed world, and both nodes must have valid saved-progress quotes. The incumbent must not itself serve that milestone. Reconsideration reruns the existing priority ladder and switches only when both its forced goal and selected technology equal that same milestone. Earlier priorities retain their places; another fallback cannot replace the incumbent. Missing or false host eligibility leaves the path alone. An already selected milestone does not issue another order.

Revelation and instantaneous eligibility are separate transient observations, including lists identical to generated rules. Missing, older or rejected exports clear them. They are rebuilt after loading a save. Native eligibility does not constrain hypothetical prerequisite searches. Ordinary worlds have no host facts and retain their research action contract.

The model previously refused every Research action while research was active. With two valid host banks and a legal destination, native switching now banks the incumbent's current progress in that mirror's quote map and restores the destination's own saved progress, without another boost or transferring science between nodes. Speculative clones retain independent banks. This supports observed native switching; it does not implement general research switching for ordinary model games.
