# A revealed launch technology can replace an obsolete native research path

Native Deity/Online Korea Science loss `civvis-20261105T103825Z` (metadata revision `5cbca14b`) demonstrates a research-slot problem after revelation. The journal requested Advanced AI at turn 176 toward Smart Materials while Smart Materials was still hidden. The native operation's `GetResearchPath` studied Seasteads first. At 179/0 Seasteads was owned, Smart Materials was explicitly revealed with only Seasteads as prerequisite and `can_research: true`, but the automatically queued Advanced AI had started with zero invested science. At 180 it held 383.773 science. The planner waited for that node to finish before selecting Smart Materials at 183. These are captured observations, not a counterfactual four-turn victory gain: Advanced AI could still be needed later, the Offworld route was not yet revealed, and this world lost at 190.

The separate recent world `105753` completed Earth, Moon and Mars at 162, 171 and 181, then lost at 188 without observed Smart Materials. Its only observed Future Era row was Advanced Power Cells. That is a non-firing case for this change; do not infer or expose its hidden prerequisites.

The new `revealed-launch-tech-takes-the-slot` treatment is off by default. A committed late launch chain may reconsider an active research path only when its next milestone has an explicitly observed, complete prerequisite list, is legal now, and has a valid native saved-progress quote. The incumbent must have a valid quote too and must not itself serve that milestone. The existing priority ladder is rerun, and a switch is emitted only when both its forced goal and selected technology equal that same revealed milestone. Earlier defensive, war, rush and other research priorities retain their places; reconsideration cannot switch to an unrelated fallback. An already selected milestone does not issue another order on replan.

Revelation membership is tracked independently of differences from generated rules: an observed list identical to our generated list is still an observation. Missing, old or rejected exports clear this metadata. It is transient host metadata, rebuilt after loading a save; ordinary model worlds have none.

The model previously refused every `Research` action while research was active. The native adapter can now switch when both valid host banks exist and the destination is legal. It banks the incumbent's current progress in that mirror's quote map and restores the destination's own observed saved progress, without another boost or transferring the incumbent's science. Speculative clones retain independent banks. Ordinary worlds without both quotes keep the previous refusal contract. This is support for observed native switching, not a general implementation of research switching in ordinary model games.
