# Conquest readiness: defensive war must have an executable purpose

## Status

Experimental work in progress, not a strength promotion. No Native victory or
highest-difficulty win is established by this study. All victory conditions,
difficulty settings, controller pins and the live force bundle stay unchanged.

## Exact controls before interpretation

The baseline is production-equivalent to `43f33edd80d09c5a6e09349aa7ee09bbd7355502`
(#4090). Its cached compiled source tree is
`cb721b4ed4aeae53d8d4dd22d09abcb16b860ec0`; every one of its 2,757 tracked blobs
was reverified. The only differences from that main are four evaluation
documents and one `cfg(test)` file. No production difference is substituted for
an exact source identity.

Four deliberately selected, previously observed losing maps are diagnosis
controls, not a fresh strength sample. Each runs once unwrapped and once through
a richer read-only observer. All eight complete applied-action and focal-turn
snapshot streams reproduce their saved originals byte for byte. All four
traced journals also match. Terminal results match except the expected observer
and source-equivalence metadata. None of these journals reports a truncated
turn; ring evictions do not remove the separately streamed evidence.

The observer records the raw public player projection before controller rule
hints, then the final plan, complete objective requirements and force assignments.
Its raw city-defense estimates are not assumed to be the controller's final
estimates. Public enemy military power is explicitly retained by the player-view
projection; an unsupported hidden-power explanation is rejected.

These wars are described as defensive: soldiers condemn the founder's spreaders,
without needing a staged siege. The trigger, however, only requires a visible
spreader within six tiles of a city. The ordinary condemnation step only acts
on a spreader sharing or adjacent to a soldier's tile, and the field-destruction
board excludes religious units. Merely declaring does not establish an
executable condemnation. This is a purpose/execution gap, not proof that the
wars had no benefit: wartime can deter spreaders independently. Later Recovery
and defeat are correlations, not isolated causal effects of these declarations.

The existing wall-priced selector preserves its ordinary target fallback, and
the board feasibility gate explicitly exempts an assigned Domination lane so
it can mobilize. Neither supplies evidence for blindly removing declaration
readiness gates. The separate prewar-siege experiment #4036 is not duplicated.

## Registered intervention and acceptance

Test a narrow version-two repair: forecast the declaration, movement and
condemnation against the available player view; open only when a healthy,
unspent military interceptor can legally execute that condemnation immediately,
and execute it in the same opening. Preserve version one's historical behavior,
all existing power, treasury, income, war-count, founder and cooldown gates, and
the independent emergency match-point interception, which runs first.

Known-case replays measure mechanism only. Before candidate compilation or any
new outcome, eight fresh seeds were registered, four per difficulty:

- Immortal: 49293455737, 47651167238, 46732028391, 45312861349.
- Deity: 49478848256, 43663176127, 49220027834, 40171860454.

Both arms use the unchanged four-major, six-city-state, 60-by-38 Pangaea,
Online-speed, all-victories, 250-turn-cap probe profile. Opponents retain their
difficulty bonuses and the focal controller retains the same handicap exemption.
This is a controlled engine stress test, not a Firaxis Native verification game.
Complete every registered pair to natural terminal or the existing cap; never
replace a seed or stop because an intermediate outcome looks favorable.

Primary outcomes are focal Domination wins, all focal wins and Religious losses.
Secondary outcomes include condemnation execution, declaration timing, observed
foreign-origin city holdings, and city count, production and power at turns 60
and 90. Production promotion requires at least one fresh candidate Domination
win, no fewer total focal wins, and no more Religious losses. Failing that gate
does not become a strength improvement because a regression test passes.
