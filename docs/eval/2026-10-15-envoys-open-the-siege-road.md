The default-off `envoys-open-the-siege-road` treatment gives an affordable suzerainty package priority over the ordinary one-envoy economic ranking when a current or previous-turn Stage hold names that city-state as blocking a siege against an existing major enemy. The unit must still belong to us, the target must still be an enemy major's city, the route must still name that blocker, and sending influence must be currently legal. Existing passage, war with the minor, a stale hold or a dead unit disqualifies it. Merely tying a rival's delegation does not buy passage.
