# Forward airfield preparation: registered descriptive support-chain audit

Use the complete fixed eight Emperor material starts and sixteen Immortal
Culture-air starts from the merged forward-airstrip census. Keep the cohorts
separate. Their outcomes and the terrain census are already known. The
exploratory Rio 157 and Uruk 165 witnesses have also been inspected: Rio has
positive income and offered Encampments, while Uruk has negative income and
no observed support-chain offer. This is a descriptive feasibility audit,
not a blind or causal strength experiment.

Retain all 254 blocked capital-turns (70 Emperor, 184 Immortal) from the
authoritative corrected census. Deduplicate their actual Native turn/frame
states only for reading city menus; do not replace or omit an unfavorable
capital or game. Keep all zero-neutral-site cases. Classify original capitals
and plot eligibility exactly as the preceding census did, and retain its source
and run-file hashes. No later Native observation moves backward in time.

For each blocked capital-turn record treasury, current income, unit maintenance,
Flight/Military Engineering and the existing neutral/owned terrain candidates.
For every own city retain population, district capacity, production yield,
current queue/progress, completed/pillaged Encampment and prerequisite buildings,
and actual offered Encampment/Barracks/Stable/Armory/Engineer production or
purchase entries. Missing menu, cost, progress or turns remain unknown.

The current export and shipped ProductionPanel use `c` for cost and `p` for
host turns left. District `n`/`s` describe placement offers/sites; `n` is not a
turn estimate. Check these semantics against the installed primary UI before
forecasting. Do not invent an unoffered purchase or call a menu quote a future
permission. Distances from a producer to terrain are geometry, not safe routes
or an Engineer's actual travel time. Keep resource, upkeep and treasury limits.

Report the next actually offered stage and the missing chain separately.
Any future-stage cost/time illustration must be labeled a model estimate and
retain its primary rules, speed, production and discount assumptions. It is
not host completion or evidence that a project would beat a victory clock.
Use every distinct captured read for the earliest blocked frame in each
affected game to corroborate actual city menus and state; preserve missing or
ambiguous evidence rather than substituting a later favorable frame.

Do not change gameplay or runtime settings in this audit. A construction policy
is conditional on finding a bounded, useful preparation chain and would require
its own default-off mechanism, genuine failing/passing controls, actual changed
Native proposals and whole-game validation. The running Slinger trial retains
its own exclusive lease; do not overlap it with another live screen.
