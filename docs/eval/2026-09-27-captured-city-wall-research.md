# Captured-city wall research preparation

Default-off experiment, PR #3800. Native promotion is not established.

The verified Prince Gran Colombia game captured Székesfehérvár at turn41 and lost it to a military capture at76. Masonry was boosted by25 but did not complete until74; walls first appeared in the legal production menu at74, with a five-turn estimate, and were selected at75. The preserved prefix has12 free research slots from45 through73. These are observed choices, not a counterfactual survival result.

The existing wall research helper already serves Domination and Culture. It requests Masonry during major-war Recovery or, for Domination, when a visible competitive melee attacker has a short route to an unwalled city. This experiment preserves that helper and adds a fallback for an owned unwalled city whose major founder remains alive and at war with us. Only an explicit active Domination target, victory planning, and the optional gene can activate it. Legacy mode and ongoing research keep their existing choices. No movement, threat, production or native-control policy changes.
