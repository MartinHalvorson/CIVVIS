# Universities before the forge — candidate qualification

`universities-before-the-forge` is a new, default-OFF, unpriced opt-in.
It releases a Science city's Industrial Zone/Workshop producer claim when a
legal University (including a civilization replacement) fits within 24
Standard turns / 12 Online turns. The ordinary production and Campus-building
selectors then decide the queue. It does not directly force or reserve a build.

An existing industrial head finishing within one turn stays first. Other active
heads, Recovery, the locally threatened city, recent attacks, completed or
unavailable Universities and Rocketry leave the industrial rules unchanged.
Factory and power-plant coordination is unchanged. No runtime policy, native
rules or operator force lists are modified.
