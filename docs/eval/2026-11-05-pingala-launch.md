## Policy contract

The new version considers a currently queued Spaceport or launch project,
not an idle city or merely available site. Keep an existing Spaceport/project
post, and Curator's housed works. Exchange posts only with ordinary Magnus;
never evict another specialist or defensive governor. Both governors retain
an assignment. The established modeled aggregate science/culture reading
must not fall. This is an analysis-world constraint, not a native yield claim.

Retain residence, safety, major-war, Recovery, asynchronous replan and horizon
guards. Native establishment budgets at least five turns; the existing Online
duration table also says five. Space Initiative does not accelerate Spaceport
construction. A positive fixture must price the project's existing production
multiplier when assigning its completion time.
