# Fund stalled Domination upgrades

Native082146 turn 99 held 21 Gold at +5.82812 per turn while Professional Army was already slotted. The host quoted 60 Gold for an Archer upgrade and 95 for several Spearmen, with insufficient Gold as the reported block. Native083707 turn 195 independently held 53 Gold at +8 per turn and 123 total unit upkeep with Force Modernization slotted. Host quotes included 75 for Pike and Shot to AT Crew, 105 for Field Cannon to Machine Gun, and 115 for Trebuchet to Artillery. Conscription was available in both cases but absent from the active deck. A Gold refusal can precede other native checks; these observations do not prove every quoted upgrade was otherwise legal.

The existing maintenance intervention starts at negative income or retains a discount already equipped. Positive but inadequate income misses that entry point. The ordinary policy replacement rule also protects every desired card, so raising a discount in the list alone does not guarantee it can occupy a filled military slot.

The candidate uses existing Domination upgrade needs, which exclude Recon, naval/air units and successors lacking the required strategic stock. During a major war or a named Conquest offensive, it asks for maintenance relief when current Gold plus four turns of positive income cannot cover the two cheapest eligible upgrades and the existing emergency reserve. Native Gold quotes take precedence over modeled prices. It only requests relief when a current unit has upkeep left to reduce. Conscription or its available successor may replace ordinary military cards; upgrade discounts, loyalty safeguards and culture-defense cards remain protected. Native upgrade legality, actual purchases and reserve rules are unchanged.

The regression failed before implementation because Conscription was not slotted; two negative tests passed. After implementation, all 12 modernization tests passed, covering an active war and a staged offensive, upkeep reduction with Professional Army retained, adequate cash/income, ordinary peace, another victory lane, a single eligible unit and no unlocked successor. The existing native-quote, native-refusal and actual-upgrade budget tests also pass.
