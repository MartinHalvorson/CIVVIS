# Royal Society serves the launch — provisional experiment

The independent opt-in `royal-society-serves-the-launch` extends the science drive's existing Royal Society credit to the Government Plaza city when another city holds the launch commitment. Its numerical credit is unchanged. It leaves the legacy launch-city result intact, rather than double-paying it.

This is motivated by saved native observations, not a win-rate result. A read-only census of20 completed ordinary Indonesia lane games through `civvis-20261106T032008Z` found9 Royal Society build offers and no recorded completed Royal Society. The runs used different worlds, sources and effective settings, so that census is descriptive. In `civvis-20261105T215700Z`, actual captured reads at149/frame1 show Lampung offering Royal Society at9 turns while a Research Lab had5 turns remaining. Majapahit held the committed Spaceport. The reported effective genotype included science-drive v1. The outcome was a rival Science victory at193; these facts do not prove that Royal Society would have changed it.

The source explains a specific missing price: both drive versions put the Royal Society credit behind a launch-city check, while the Science Plaza shortlist stops after Ancestral Hall and Intelligence Agency. Royal Society's shipped modifier is player-wide: `DLC/Expansion2/Data/Expansion1_Buildings.xml:318`, `MODIFIER_PLAYER_ADJUST_UNIT_PROJECT_PERCENT`, amount2 at623. Its existing builder contribution model and the native command bridge are separate from this experiment; PR4692 adds dispatch of an already planned contribution, not Royal Society's production value.

The new credit requires an explicitly assigned Science seat, an active existing science drive, an owned Government Plaza city, a standing/placed/queued Spaceport commitment, and a finite positive native Royal Society build quote. The quote must finish before the existing known support horizon (turn limit or a met rival's observable expedition arrival). Missing native quotes preserve the old value, including on simulator-only boards. Both drive versions retain their original local result. No queue, Builder, contribution, mission or research choice is directly forced.

This reuses a legacy ability-option credit; it does not forecast actual Builder availability, travel, charges or resulting project production. Those are material limitations when judging an action change. Caller eligibility and queue/displacement code are unchanged, but changed rankings can still have opportunity costs. In particular, do not infer that delaying a nearly complete Research Lab is beneficial merely because the new credit is positive.

Validation is pending. The append-point suite passes16 tests. Seven focused Rust tests cover default-off behavior, both drive versions, local-credit compatibility, absent/invalid native quotes, lane/ownership/item/drive restrictions, pad/Plaza commitments and the known deadline. Their first build found fixture type errors; those fixtures were corrected without changing the production policy, and the corrected tests must pass before qualification. Controlled repeated captured-input queries must demonstrate and audit any emitted-order changes before this experiment is ready. No firing waiver or strength rating is requested.

No native game, harness, saved continuation or game-lock waiter was launched or queued for this experiment. Native validation still requires the operator's explicit approval. The gene remains off by default; this document does not establish improved native execution, throughput or victories.
