# Royal Society serves the launch — provisional experiment

The independent opt-in `royal-society-serves-the-launch` extends the science drive's existing Royal Society credit to the Government Plaza city when another city holds the launch commitment. Its numerical credit is unchanged. It leaves the legacy launch-city result intact, rather than double-paying it.

This is motivated by saved native observations, not a win-rate result. A read-only census of20 completed ordinary Indonesia lane games through `civvis-20261106T032008Z` found9 Royal Society build offers and no recorded completed Royal Society. The runs used different worlds, sources and effective settings, so that census is descriptive. In `civvis-20261105T215700Z`, actual captured reads at149/frame1 show Lampung offering Royal Society at9 turns while a Research Lab had5 turns remaining. Majapahit held the committed Spaceport. The reported effective genotype included science-drive v1. The outcome was a rival Science victory at193; these facts do not prove that Royal Society would have changed it.

The source explains a specific missing price: both drive versions put the Royal Society credit behind a launch-city check, while the Science Plaza shortlist stops after Ancestral Hall and Intelligence Agency. Royal Society's shipped modifier is player-wide: `DLC/Expansion2/Data/Expansion1_Buildings.xml:318`, `MODIFIER_PLAYER_ADJUST_UNIT_PROJECT_PERCENT`, amount2 at623. Its existing builder contribution model and the native command bridge are separate from this experiment; PR4692 adds dispatch of an already planned contribution, not Royal Society's production value.

The new credit requires an explicitly assigned Science seat, an active existing science drive, an owned Government Plaza city, a standing/placed/queued Spaceport commitment, and a finite positive native Royal Society build quote. The quote must finish before the existing known support horizon (turn limit or a met rival's observable expedition arrival). Missing native quotes preserve the old value, including on simulator-only boards. Both drive versions retain their original local result. No queue, Builder, contribution, mission or research choice is directly forced.

This reuses a legacy ability-option credit; it does not forecast actual Builder availability, travel, charges or resulting project production. Those are material limitations when judging an action change. Caller eligibility and queue/displacement code are unchanged, but changed rankings can still have opportunity costs. In particular, do not infer that delaying a nearly complete Research Lab is beneficial merely because the new credit is positive.

Local validation passes16 append-point tests and all7 focused Rust tests (`CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2 CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG=0 cargo test --profile dev --locked --lib royal_society_serves_the_launch`). The first build found fixture type errors, which were corrected without changing the production policy. The focused tests cover default-off behavior, both drive versions, local-credit compatibility, absent/invalid native quotes, lane/ownership/item/drive restrictions, pad/Plaza commitments and the known deadline. These helper tests do not establish a useful production change.

The first exploratory order probe found **no changed orders or internal native actions** across14 captured frontiers from8 completed ordinary Indonesia runs (28 cold OFF/ON requests). The frozen source was `fa087cd4f4e7b890766a7b433f0877db659287d8`; its unoptimized dev executable SHA256 was `9997388953504913f75b6290360bba21033c45200d24566f2b984f2f55ff0dfa`. An independent saved-result audit checked all476 actual recaptured reads against the fixed input bytes, equal argv except the target switch and normalized mirror path, and effective genomes differing only by this gene. This held one actual native frontier fixed at every read in a later program. It did not reproduce the original read timing or persistent native AI history, use repeated OFF/ON/ON/OFF qualification, execute the commands in Civilization VI, or measure wins or runtime throughput.

The candidate is **rejected for advancement and closed unmerged**. Its firing gate still reports this new gene missing; no waiver is requested. The negative probe does not justify increasing the credit to obtain a firing result. City explanations include protected science queues, military reservations, Spy denial, Workshop and Builder choices. Those explanations identify possible competing decisions; they do not establish that every case reached this pricing helper. Diagnose the actual caller and opportunity costs before revising or advancing the experiment.

A separate test-only initial-mirror score diagnostic completed14 cases. The helper returned the added400 credit in12; the other2 retained the existing local credit. In11 cases that changed the final scalar score, in1 an earlier refusal kept both scores at−10,000, and in2 the legacy score stayed unchanged. Royal Society ranked first in none. This diagnostic used a recorded external test-only patch over the frozen source; the temporary test source was restored exactly. It is not full-turn staging or original private-history parity. For Lampung at150, Royal Society rose from0 to28.57 while the Research Lab scored148.92, supporting retention of that Lab queue rather than a forced switch.

The experiment is being closed without merging its implementation. The sampled behavior does not support advancing this fixed-credit relocation, and the evidence does not justify retuning it merely to pass firing. This does not establish that Royal Society is worthless: a future valuation would need an actual Builder/project job and its opportunity costs. Earlier Campus and Library scheduling offers a more immediate research direction.

No native game, harness, saved continuation or game-lock waiter was launched or queued for this experiment. Native validation still requires the operator's explicit approval. The gene remains off by default; this document does not establish improved native execution, throughput or victories.
