# Candidate contract: arm a healthy Slinger before a watched war

Default-off candidate `capital-watch-arms-the-slinger` reuses the existing
peaceful-major capital watch to consider one healthy Slinger-to-Archer
upgrade before the named-offensive/wartime modernization pass applies.
The exploratory Native start 193342 shows a Slinger at 100 HP and 38 Gold
on turn 20 with a 30-Gold Archer quote. It remains healthy at turn 21 with
44 Gold and no exported upgrade refusal, moves outside friendly territory
by 22, falls to 2 HP by 24, returns to friendly territory and upgrades then.
This is a hypothesis about missed preparation, not evidence that changing it
would save the capital or win. Ordinary modernization also needs a named
Conquest or active war; the fixed reserve is not the only possible blocker.

Preserve current emergency city purchases, appointed war budgets, running
major wars, other victory lanes and civilian-bound guards. Require an actual
upgrade offer and normal action legality, healthy HP, positive projected net
income after the existing discounted upkeep calculation, and a remaining
upkeep reserve. Consider at most one proposal per turn. Keep the original
wall policy's research/production behavior unchanged when its flag is off;
the new flag must independently observe its warning without implicitly
enabling walls. Record selected proposals with a distinct journal prefix.
Do not relax host restrictions or count an accepted local action as Native
execution.

Before selecting positive results, compare all sixteen completed trial
starts from PR #4141. Select each turn's first actual decision through turn
60 when its observed Slinger has HP >=75 and its exported upgrade bill is
affordable, plus each game's first/final actual decision and all actual
Slinger upgrade-order frames. Retain refusal/territory/missing-input cases,
deduplicate frames and evaluate every distinct captured read. Use each
actual played genome, and vary only the new flag between adapter arms.
Fresh queries do not reproduce the entire historical controller memory.

Register eight model games at seeds 59006400–59006407: Domination assignment,
four players, 60x38 Pangaea, six city-states, Online speed, 300 turns, all six
victory conditions, Immortal handicaps on three Firaxis-mix rival chairs.
Pin supported screenable genes from the first unarmed Native start after this
task began (2026-10-25 21:09:51 UTC); record its full profile and every
unrepresented treatment. Also replay the same eight worlds in matched arms,
holding all rival starting genomes fixed and changing only the measured
seat's new bit. Retain full action/world comparisons and original-sample
reproduction controls. These models do not substitute for Native wins.

After shipping and actual Native driver/binary/input qualification, a separate
prospective trial may use sixteen future starts with deterministic tag/gene/
actual-difficulty/lane assignments. Record its protocol and exclusive lease
before activation. Preserve every assigned failure and continuation, stop
assignment at sixteen without replacement, and collect terminal outcomes
after the cutoff. Do not overlap another active lease or rearm an old trial.

Run focused genuine failing/passing controls, full required Rust/repository
checks and an actual action-firing comparison. No default promotion or
strength claim is authorized by unit success, independent outcome contrasts,
or fresh adapter proposals alone.
