# Science launch deadlines

Targeted science selects the legal Spaceport city with the shortest remaining
project build time. The comparison includes banked progress and the city's
item-specific production multiplier. Raw production alone can choose a fresh
launch over a nearly complete banked project in another city. The unassigned
controller keeps its historical raw-production ranking.

Science Drive 2 no longer awards its extra support-building or district bonus
when that item finishes at or after a known rival's Exoplanet arrival, or the
configured turn limit. The observed flight deadline uses current distance and
speed, so subsequent acceleration can make it earlier. Unmet rivals supply no
private information. Launch projects and Spaceports keep their race priority.
Ordinary support valuation and urgent defence remain available.

Tests exercise the actual launch production pass with two Spaceports and a
nearly completed banked project, and a one-turn rival flight with and without
contact. The existing science-drive, pillaged-pad recovery, tech-chain and
launch-horizon tests remain in the focused suite. These are scheduling fixes;
completed fixed-controller live cohorts are still required to measure wins.
